# Recording protocol

The whole pipeline is keyed on one clip = one pick-and-place. Thirty clips took about
fifteen minutes to record.

**Scene.** A plain table, a bowl (any small bowl, a dark one tracks best against a light
table), and a plate about 30 cm away. Keep the rest of the table clear.

**Camera.** Phone held or propped above the table looking roughly straight down, far
enough that the whole bowl-to-plate path and your hand stay in frame. Fixed position
for the whole clip. Landscape, 1080p, 30 fps. Good even light, no hard shadows from the
phone.

**Action, per clip.**
1. Start with your right hand flat and open at the edge of the frame, not over the bowl.
2. Move over the bowl, lower, pinch the rim between thumb and index finger.
3. Lift, carry to the plate, lower, open the fingers to release.
4. Lift your hand back up and hold still for one second.

Four to eight seconds per clip. Use one hand only and keep the fingers visible; the
grip signal comes from the thumb and index tip.

**Variation.** Move the bowl and plate to different spots between clips, vary the
carry height and speed, approach from different sides. The retargeting re-anchors
every clip onto the sim object positions, so variety in paths matters more than
precision.

**Transfer.** AirDrop the clips to the Mac into `data/raw/` as `clip_01.mov` ... Then
`make hands`.
